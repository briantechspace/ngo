About Our Organization

Who We Are

Doorway to Acceptance (DTA) is a Kenyan non-profit dedicated to creating pathways toward safety, dignity, leadership, and economic independence for girls and women.

We recognize that gender inequality and climate change are deeply interconnected. Rather than responding only after violence occurs, DTA invests in root-cause prevention through education, climate-smart enterprises, and community-wide safeguarding.

Read Our Full Story
👁

Our Vision

A world where every girl and woman lives free from violence, exercises her rights, thrives economically, and leads climate resilience.

🎯

Our Mission

To prevent SGBV by empowering girls and women through education, leadership, climate action, sustainable livelihoods, and advocacy.

⛓

Our Theory of Change

When girls receive quality education, understand their rights, access economic opportunities, and participate in climate solutions, they gain the independence needed to resist exploitation and shape their own futures.

Strategic Framework

Our Pillars of Change

A comprehensive preventative framework centered on five interconnected pillars of community action and development.

📘

1. Education & Life Skills

Knowledge is the first line of defense.

Mentorship, SRHR awareness, financial and digital literacy, career guidance, and civic engagement for girls.

Goal: Nurture resilient, confident girls who advocate for their rights.
💼

2. Economic Empowerment

Pathways to financial independence.

Entrepreneurship training, savings groups, business incubation, and green enterprises like beekeeping and aquaculture.

Goal: Empower women and youth to achieve economic self-reliance.
🌱

3. Climate Action

Women-led environmental solutions.

Nature-based solutions, tree growing, ecosystem restoration, sustainable farming, and clean energy adoption.

Goal: Build climate-resilient communities led by women environmental stewards.
🛡️

4. Safe Communities & Advocacy

Preventing violence before it occurs.

Transforming harmful norms through community awareness, school safeguarding, positive masculinity, and referral pathways.

Goal: Build inclusive, safe societies where SGBV is prevented.
🤝

5. Survivor Support & Reintegration

Walking alongside survivors on their healing journey.

Facilitating access to psychosocial counseling, medical care, legal aid, skills development, and community reintegration.

Goal: Ensure survivors rebuild their lives with dignity, confidence, and hope.
Holistic Model

Our Integrated Approach

Education builds confidence. Economic empowerment creates independence. Climate action strengthens resilience. By addressing these areas together, DTA creates lasting, community-driven solutions.

Interconnected Challenge

Why Gender and Climate?

Climate change and gender inequality reinforce each other. Women and girls experience its impacts first through food insecurity, lost livelihoods, water scarcity, and displacement.

🌦️

When droughts strike, women bear the burden of agricultural failure and hunger.

🌊

When floods displace families, girls are vulnerable to school dropout and early marriage.

🌪️

When livelihoods disappear, vulnerability to exploitation and abuse escalates.

Empowering Women as Stewards

Investing in women as environmental stewards and entrepreneurs simultaneously advances gender equality, climate resilience, and sustainable development.

"Gender equality and climate action are inseparable. Empowered women lead the healthiest ecosystems."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our grassroots programs, donation transparency, and survivor support.

Every shilling is channeled directly into grassroots community interventions. Over 85% of funds go straight to program delivery—including sanitary towel distribution, climate-resilient tree nurseries, trauma counseling sessions, and legal aid referrals for SGBV survivors in Kenya.

Yes. Doorway to Acceptance (DTA) is a fully registered Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) in Kenya (Reg No: OP.218/051/20-291/12480). Upon donating via M-PESA or bank transfer, you immediately receive a verifiable digital Tax Certificate and Official Receipt.

When you enter your phone number and contribution amount, an instant secure STK prompt pops up on your Safaricom phone. You simply enter your M-PESA PIN. The payment is processed via our licensed gateway (UpesiPay) with zero transaction fees deducted from your intended donation.

We strictly adhere to international safeguarding standards. All survivor cases are handled with absolute confidentiality by trained social workers, providing emergency safe shelter referrals, clinical medical care, and pro-bono legal advocacy without exposing survivor identities.
